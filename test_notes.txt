Basic verification checklist

1. Confirmed that the corpus contains 10 finance/banking documents.
2. Preprocessing uses lowercase conversion, non-alphabetic character removal,
   English stop-word removal, and Porter stemming.
3. TF-IDF is generated with scikit-learn's TfidfVectorizer.
4. Query ranking uses cosine similarity.
5. The sample queries and reported scores are taken from the assignment documentation.
6. Run `python run_project.py` after installing requirements to verify execution.
